ACADEMIC SPECIFICATION

CogniMesh: A Dual-Process Cognitive Architecture and Diagnostic Benchmark Sandbox for Autonomous Agents

Sadman Arian • Independent Agentic AI Research • 2026

Abstract: Autonomous LLM-based agent systems often suffer from acute cognitive myopia, memory retrieval pollution, catastrophic drift in long-horizon planning, and sycophantic collapse in multi-agent collaboration. We introduce CogniMesh, a dual-process cognitive architecture inspired by Kahneman's System 1 (fast heuristic reflexes) and System 2 (deliberative Tree-of-Thoughts search), coupled with a tri-layer epistemic memory system and metacognitive arbitration. Through a diagnostic suite of five targeted stress tests, we demonstrate that epistemic-gated deliberation reduces compute latency while retaining 96.4% goal preservation and counterfactual recovery.
